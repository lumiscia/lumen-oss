Text underline, strikethrough and highlight `extent` now measure fractions of the text's width, as documented, not of its bytes. Animated reveals of text with non-ASCII or mixed-width characters, such as `"extent": "=vec2(0, frame / 30)"` over "Mi casa — 你好", now sweep at an even pace instead of speeding up over wide characters, and an ellipsis counts as part of the width. A text node that draws more than 2048 glyphs, counting a stroked glyph twice and each decoration once, now fails to render with an error naming the node and the count, instead of silently dropping the rest and disabling cached output for the whole composition. Whitespace is not drawn, so it no longer counts. Text whose layout and decorations are constant allocates and draws only the instances it needs. Glyph-atlas entries for whitespace and glyphs that cannot use a distance field are released once unused, and the overfull-atlas retry check follows the glyphs the atlas will actually place. For Rust: `TextSystem::prepare_glyphs` and `prepare_stroked_glyphs` no longer take `max_glyphs`.
