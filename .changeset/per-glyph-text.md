Text nodes take `glyph_offset`, `glyph_scale`, `glyph_rotation` and `glyph_opacity`, evaluated for every glyph to stagger glyphs, words and lines apart. Inside them, expressions can read the `glyph`, `word` and `line` index of the glyph being drawn and the `glyph_count`, `word_count` and `line_count` of the text, such as `"glyph_opacity": "=tween(0, 1, 10 + glyph * 2, 12)"`; elsewhere those globals are an error. Each glyph scales and turns about the middle of its advance and line, before the text's own transform, and fades before its `opacity`. `@lumiscia/lumen-shared` exports them as `glyph`, `word`, `line`, `glyphCount`, `wordCount` and `lineCount`.
