`lumen-server` keeps uploaded media and resolves `asset:<id>` references against it. `POST /media` with a single `PUT` or a multipart upload, and `POST /media/url`, take the same requests and answer the same shapes as the hosted API, so the SDK's `uploadMedia` and `createUrlMedia` work against it unchanged. Each file is checked once its bytes are complete, by opening it with the decoders renders use, and the media reports its `kind` (`image`, `video`, `audio` or `font`) and `metadata`, or `rejected` with a `rejectionReason`. `POST /renders` refuses a composition naming media that is missing, expired, pending, rejected or of the wrong kind with `422` and `asset.not_found` or `asset.wrong_kind` diagnostics, and `POST /renders/check` reports the same. Media is kept in `--media-dir` for `--media-ttl` (24 hours by default), up to `--media-max-bytes` per file, and a render keeps the assets it uses until it ends. At most `--media-max-pending` media (100 by default) wait for their content at once, and `POST /media` past that answers `503` with `media_upload_limit` until one is checked, deleted or expires.
